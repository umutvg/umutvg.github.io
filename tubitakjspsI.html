About the Workshop

The workshop Fukaya Categories, Sheaves and Quantization will take place at Koç University in İstanbul from March 15 to March 19, 2027. Organized under the framework of the bilateral TÜBİTAK–JSPS Joint Research Project between Koç University (Türkiye) and Kyoto University (Japan), this workshop brings together researchers working in symplectic geometry, microlocal sheaf theory, and homological mirror symmetry.

The primary aim of the workshop is to foster international collaboration and present recent breakthroughs regarding the structure and invariants of Fukaya categories, sheaves, and quantization.
