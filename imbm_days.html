About the Workshop

From classical foundations to the disproof of the Nearby Lagrangian Conjecture

Organized as part of the revitalized IMBM Days series of the Istanbul Center for Mathematical Sciences (IMBM) and hosted at Boğaziçi University, this two-day workshop brings together mathematicians in Istanbul and Türkiye around one of symplectic topology's most celebrated problems: the Nearby Lagrangian Conjecture (NLC).

Opening with the history and the background of the conjecture and closing with an overview of its disproof, the middle three lectures will build the geometric and homotopical machinery needed to bridge the gap.
